# PenSign

Add your signature to any PDF in seconds. Draw once, place anywhere, and share flattened signed documents offline. No account needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it legally binding?**
PenSign adds your drawn signature to a document. Whether an electronic signature is accepted depends on the recipient and local law.

**Are my documents or signatures uploaded to the cloud?**
No. All processing, document viewing, and signature generation happen locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Open **Profile → Restore Purchases**. If you previously purchased PenSign Pro with your Apple Account, it will be restored immediately.

**How do I delete saved signatures?**
Go to **Profile → Manage Signatures**. You can review and delete individual signatures or initials at any time.

**How do I change the app language?**
Open **Profile → Language**, select your preferred language, then close and reopen PenSign.
