# PenSign — Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 October 2026

## Documents and Signatures Stay on Your Device

Documents and signatures are processed and stored locally on your device. The app does not upload your files, signatures, or personal data to external servers or cloud services. PenSign works completely offline.

Saved signatures and initials remain in the app's secure local application storage on your device. You can view or delete your saved signatures at any time from the app's Profile screen.

Exported signed documents remain wherever you save or share them (such as the Files app or through the system share sheet); that destination's privacy practices apply.

## No Accounts and No Tracking

PenSign has no user accounts, no advertising SDKs, no analytics tools, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect or monitor your documents, signatures, or app activity.

## Purchases

Apple processes all in-app purchases and payment information. PenSign uses Apple's StoreKit framework to verify purchase status. Restoring purchases may require an internet connection to communicate directly with Apple's App Store.
