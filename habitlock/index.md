# HabitLock: Support & FAQ

Shield distracting apps until you complete a simple daily habit from your 21-day plan. Calm, honest, private, and free to try.

> **Honest Limits:** HabitLock shields the apps, categories and websites you choose. It can't lock your whole phone, and you can switch it off any time in Settings. It works because you want it to.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How does HabitLock shield apps?
HabitLock uses Apple's official Screen Time (Family Controls) API. You choose the apps, categories, and websites you want to shield. When your daily threshold is reached or your habit is pending, iOS applies a calm shield screen.

#### Can HabitLock lock my entire phone?
No. Essential apps such as Phone, Messages, and iOS Settings are never shielded. You can always make phone calls and access your device settings.

#### Can I bypass the shield?
You can switch Screen Time off at any time in **Settings > Screen Time > Apps with Screen Time Access**, or remove HabitLock. We do not pretend otherwise. HabitLock provides intentional friction—not an unbreakable prison.

#### What are Emergency Unlocks?
Life happens. Free members receive 2 emergency unlocks per month; Pro members receive 5. Tapping Emergency Unlock gives you 10 minutes of access after a calm breathing pause. Emergency unlocks reset on the first of each month and can never be bought or sold.

#### How does the 21-day habit plan work?
A plan consists of 21 completed days, not calendar days. If you miss a day, your progress is not wiped out. Timer habits gently increase in duration as you advance through weeks 1, 2, and 3.

#### Is my data sent anywhere?
No. HabitLock stores everything locally on your device within an App Group. We have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. We cannot see which apps you use or shield. The App Store privacy disclosure is **Data Not Collected**.

#### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple: **Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > HabitLock**. Cancelling stops renewal at the end of the current billing cycle; you keep Pro benefits until then.

#### I restored my device or bought a new iPhone — how do I restore Pro?
Open HabitLock → **Settings > Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account.

#### How do I export my data?
Open **Settings > Export Backup** to create a JSON backup file that you can save to Files or AirDrop.
