# Privacy Policy — HabitLock

Last updated: November 2026

At **HabitLock**, privacy is built into the architecture: the app has no account system and no backend to send data to. Our App Store privacy disclosure is **Data Not Collected**.

### 1. Data Not Collected
HabitLock does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, identifiers, diagnostics or usage data. There are no third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or advertising frameworks in the app.

### 2. Apple Screen Time & Family Controls
HabitLock uses Apple's official Screen Time (Family Controls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity) frameworks to apply shields to the apps, categories, and websites you choose.
- Apple's system handles all app selection tokens securely and privately.
- HabitLock developers never see, record, or transmit the names or contents of the apps you shield or use.
- All shield thresholds and schedules are evaluated locally on your device by iOS.

### 3. Camera
If you choose an exercise habit, the camera counts your push-ups or squats on your device. Video is never saved or sent.

### 4. On-Device Storage & App Groups
Everything the app stores — profiles, habit plans, check-ins, and emergency unlock counts — lives in local storage on your device inside a private App Group. It is never uploaded to any remote server.

### 5. Backups & iCloud Sync
The export feature writes a backup file only where you choose to save it (Files, iCloud Drive, another device). Optional iCloud sync for Pro subscribers synchronizes plan settings and history across your own devices using your private Apple ID. App selection tokens are device-specific and are never uploaded to iCloud.

### 6. Purchases & Subscriptions
Payments are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit 2. The app only asks Apple whether your subscription is active so it can unlock Pro — purchase amounts, payment methods and Apple Account details never reach us.

### 7. Notifications
If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device for habit reminders and countdown completion. No notification contains or sends personal data.

### 8. Links
The app contains links to this site and to the standard Apple terms. Opening them leaves the app and is subject to those sites' policies.

### 9. Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
