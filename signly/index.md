# Signly: Support & FAQ

Signly teaches American Sign Language (ASL) in short visual lessons, with spaced-repetition review, a phrasebook, quizzes and fingerspelling practice.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Does the camera record me?
Only if you tap **Record**: that video stays on your device. Fingerspelling feedback checks camera frames on your device and never saves them. It is practice feedback, not grading.

#### Is my data sent anywhere?
No, not to us. Signly stores everything on your device. There is no account, no server of ours and no analytics. If you choose to turn on iCloud sync (below), a copy of your progress goes to your own iCloud account, never to us. The App Store privacy disclosure is *Data Not Collected*.

#### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Signly**. Cancelling there stops the renewal at the end of the current period; you keep Pro until then.

#### I restored my device / bought a new iPhone — where is my Pro?
Open Signly → **Settings › Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account. If the subscription was purchased on another Apple Account, sign in with that account first.

#### How do I export my data?
**Settings › Export backup** writes a backup file you can save to Files or send to another device. **Settings › Import backup** brings it back — you choose whether to merge or replace.

#### Can I use Signly on my iPhone and iPad with the same progress?
Yes, for free. Turn on **Settings › Sync progress with iCloud** on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on. Your lessons, review schedule, streak and settings are then merged between your devices, and progress is never lost: the more advanced result wins for each sign, and practice days from both devices are combined. The copy lives in your own iCloud, and we never see it. Recorded videos never sync. Turning sync off removes Signly's copy from your iCloud.

#### Something isn't working — how do I report it?
Write to **shubhamn910@gmail.com** from the address you used on the App Store. Include the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what you did before the problem appeared.
