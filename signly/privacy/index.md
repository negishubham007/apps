# Privacy Policy — Signly

Last updated: November 2026

At **Signly**, privacy is built into the architecture: the app has no account system and no backend to send data to. Our App Store privacy disclosure is **Data Not Collected**.

### 1. Data Not Collected
Signly does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, identifiers, diagnostics or usage data. There are no third-party analytics, SDKs or advertising frameworks in the app.

### 2. On-Device Storage
Everything the app stores — items, settings, preferences — lives in your own storage on your device and is never uploaded. If you use the Signly widgets or the Apple Watch app, they read the same data on your own devices. No server of ours can read it.

### 3. Camera
Signly uses the camera on the letter practice screen to give fingerspelling feedback. Frames are checked on your device and never saved or sent. If you tap Record, the video of yourself stays on your device.

### 4. Backups
The export feature writes a backup file only where you choose to save it (Files, iCloud Drive, another device). We never see that file. An imported file is processed on-device and discarded.

### 5. Purchases & Subscriptions
Payments are handled entirely by Apple. The app only asks Apple whether your subscription is active so it can unlock Pro — purchase amounts, payment methods and Apple Account details never reach us.

### 6. Notifications
If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification contains or sends personal data.

### 7. Links
The app contains links to this site and to the standard Apple terms. Opening them leaves the app and is subject to those sites' policies.

### 8. Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
