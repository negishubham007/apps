# Shotproof — Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 October 2026

## Photos, Locations, and Data Stay on Your Device

All photos, GPS coordinates, addresses, job records, and company branding remain securely on your device. The app operates locally and does not upload your work photos or documents to any cloud server.

Location access is used only while the camera screen is actively taking or stamping photos ("When In Use"). Location tracking stops immediately when the camera closes.

## Network-Verified Time and Timestamp Certificates

To guarantee that photo timestamps remain accurate even if a device's clock is misconfigured:
- When connected to the internet, the app performs a lightweight check to standard public web servers (`apple.com` or `cloudflare.com`) to verify UTC time. No photos, files, or personal details are transmitted during this check.
- When generating optional independent timestamp certificates (RFC 3161), only the cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprint (hash) and a random nonce are sent to a trusted public Time Stamping Authority (such as DigiCert, Sectigo, or FreeTSA). Your photos, image contents, locations, and identity are never sent.

## No Accounts, No Ads, and No Tracking

Shotproof does not use user accounts, third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking cookies. We do not monitor your jobs, clients, or usage.

## Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely by Apple via StoreKit. Restoring purchases connects directly to Apple's App Store.
