## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I restore my Shotproof Pro purchase?
1. Open **Shotproof** and go to **Profile**.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. Apple StoreKit will verify your subscription and unlock Pro features immediately.

### How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
All subscription plans (monthly and yearly with 1-month free trial) are managed through Apple:
1. Open iOS **Settings**.
2. Tap your **Apple Account** name at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions > Shotproof**.
4. Tap **Cancel Subscription** (or **Cancel Free Trial**).
Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being billed.

### How do I delete my data?
Shotproof stores all job photos and project logs locally:
- To delete individual photos or jobs: Select them in the Gallery tab, or open a job in the Jobs tab, and tap Delete.
- To erase all data: Delete the Shotproof app from your device.

### Will the stamp stay on my photos when exported to PC or Mac?
Yes. Shotproof permanently burns the watermark into a standard sRGB JPEG file. Opening the photo in Windows Explorer, Mac Preview, or third-party web portals displays the stamp permanently.
