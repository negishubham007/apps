## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I restore my Shrinkwise Pro purchase?
1. Open **Shrinkwise** and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. Apple StoreKit will verify your active subscription and unlock unlimited storage cleaning and video shrinking immediately.

### How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly through Apple:
1. Open iOS **Settings**.
2. Tap your **Apple Account** name at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions > Shrinkwise**.
4. Tap **Cancel Subscription**.
Access continues until the end of the current billing cycle. Shrinkwise Pro is billed on monthly or yearly plans without introductory free trials.

### How does Swipe Review work?
Swipe Review lets you clean your photo library one photo at a time. Swipe a photo left to stage it for removal, or swipe right to keep it. Staged photos remain in a pending queue until you tap Confirm Deletion, and you can undo any accidental swipe at any time.

### How does Live Photo to still photo conversion work?
A Live Photo contains both a full-resolution still photo and an attached 3-second companion video clip. Shrinkwise extracts and saves the still image, verifies that it is preserved in every user album the original belonged to, and moves the original Live Photo to Recently Deleted to free up significant space.

### Why has my available storage not changed immediately after cleaning?
When you delete duplicate photos, screenshots, or replace original videos, Apple Photos moves originals to your **Recently Deleted** album where they remain safe for 30 days. To free up storage space immediately, open the Apple Photos app, scroll to **Recently Deleted**, and tap **Delete All**.

### How does video compression maintain quality?
Shrinkwise utilizes Apple Silicon hardware HEVC and H.264 encoders. In "Keep Quality" mode, Shrinkwise samples video frames and tests structural similarity (SSIM) against the original. If a video cannot be compressed with high visual fidelity (SSIM index ≥ 0.98), Shrinkwise keeps your original file untouched.

### What is free and what needs Pro?
Free: the first 2 GB of savings and 50 items across cleaning and video shrinking, plus Keep quality mode for videos. Shrinkwise Pro removes the limits and adds the Balanced and Max space modes, overnight compression while charging, and bulk iCloud downloads. There is no free trial.

### How do I delete my data?
Shrinkwise maintains no user accounts, external databases, or analytics tracking. All processing occurs locally on your device. Deleting the app immediately removes all local preferences and temporary caches.
