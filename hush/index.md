# Hush: Migraine Pressure Diary — Support & FAQ

Log a migraine in two taps, get warned before pressure swings, understand your triggers, and hand your doctor a clean report. No ads, no account; your health data stays on your iPhone.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do pressure alerts work and what location is sent?
Hush uses an advance forecast to alert you when barometric pressure is predicted to drop or rise significantly (e.g. 6 hPa within 24 hours). 

To retrieve the pressure forecast, Hush requests your **approximate location** (coarse location rounded to 0.1°) and queries Apple's WeatherKit service directly. Your location is:
- Sent only to Apple WeatherKit to fetch the weather forecast.
- **Never linked to your health data, symptoms, or headache diary.**
- Never stored on external servers or tracked over time.
- If you prefer not to grant location access, you can manually select a city in **Settings > Pressure Alerts**.

#### Why do pressure alerts need a forecast?
Barometric pressure swings often trigger migraine and tension headaches hours before the front arrives. The built-in iPhone barometer measures current pressure when the app is open, but only a forecast can warn you in advance so you can hydrate, take prescribed preventive steps, or adjust your schedule.

#### Does Hush diagnose anything?
**No.** Hush is strictly a personal logging and tracking diary. It does not diagnose medical conditions, recommend medications, or promise prevention. Always discuss your headache patterns, symptoms, and medications with a qualified physician or neurologist.

#### What is the medication-overuse notice?
Hush tracks acute medication intake days against standard **ICHD-3 (International Classification of Headache Disorders)** clinical guidelines:
- **Triptans, ergots, opioids, or combination analgesics:** 10 or more days per month.
- **Simple analgesics (e.g. acetaminophen, ibuprofen, NSAIDs):** 15 or more days per month.

When your logged medication days approach or reach these thresholds, Hush displays an informational notice so you are aware of your usage. This is not a diagnosis—always review your medication frequency with your doctor.

#### How do I back up my diary?
Go to **Settings > Export & Backup**. You can export your complete diary to a standard JSON file via the iOS Share Sheet, save it to iCloud Drive, or transfer it to your computer. You can also import a backup anytime to restore your data.

#### Subscriptions: Pricing and Free Trial
Hush Pro provides advanced pressure alerts, detailed trigger and pattern insights, Apple Health cycle and sleep correlation, medication-overuse tracking, and doctor report PDF generation.
- **Yearly:** $39.99/year with a **1-month free trial** (cancel anytime during the trial at no charge).
- **Monthly:** $5.99/month (no trial).

All logging, medications, history calendar, basic monthly counts, and data export are free forever. 

To manage or cancel your subscription:
1. Open the iOS **Settings** app on your device.
2. Tap your **Apple Account** name at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions** and select **Hush**.

To restore an existing subscription, tap **Settings > Restore Purchases** inside Hush.

#### How do I change the language?
Hush is localized in 14 languages: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, and Simplified Chinese.

Hush automatically matches your iPhone's system language. To use a different language for Hush only:
1. Open iOS **Settings**.
2. Scroll down to **Apps > Hush**.
3. Tap **Preferred Language** and select your desired language.
