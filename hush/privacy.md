# Privacy Policy — Hush: Migraine Pressure Diary

Last updated: October 2026

At **Hush**, privacy is the cornerstone of our design. Health logs, symptoms, pain levels, and medication records are deeply sensitive personal data. Hush is built so that your health data stays entirely on your device.

---

### 1. Data Not Collected
Hush does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal health records, migraine entries, pain ratings, notes, or symptom logs. Our App Store privacy label is **Data Not Collected**.

### 2. On-Device Storage
- Your entire headache diary, medication list, custom symptoms, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone in an on-device App Group container (`group.com.negishubham.hush`).
- We operate no remote servers, databases, or accounts.
- Your data only leaves your device if you explicitly choose to export a backup file or share a PDF report.
- Daily check-ins and the emergency contact you enter (a name and phone number, typed or picked with the system contact picker) are stored the same way. Hush never reads your address book and never sends messages itself: in attack mode it only opens a pre-filled message that you review and send.

### 3. Apple Health Data (Read-Only)
- If you opt in to Apple Health integration (menstrual cycle and sleep analysis), Hush reads this data strictly on-device to display potential correlations with your headache days.
- Hush has read-only access: it never writes to Apple Health.
- HealthKit data is never transmitted across the network, never uploaded to third parties, and never leaves your iPhone.

### 4. Location & Barometric Pressure Forecast
- To forecast incoming air-pressure changes, Hush requests permission for your approximate location (coarse location, rounded to 0.1°).
- This approximate coordinate is sent directly to Apple's WeatherKit service solely to retrieve hourly surface air pressure forecasts.
- **Your location is never linked to your health data, never stored with migraine logs, and never logged remotely.**
- If you prefer not to share your location, you can disable location access at any time and manually enter a city name in Settings.

### 5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
- Subscriptions to Hush Pro are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit using your Apple ID account.
- Hush does not collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.

### 6. No Ads and Zero Third-Party Analytics
- Hush contains **zero third-party advertising SDKs**, zero tracking pixels, and zero behavioral telemetry frameworks.
- There are no remote profiling trackers or crash loggers sending personal information.

### 7. Children's Privacy
Hush is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. All data remains exclusively on the local device.

### 8. Changes to This Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated revision date. Any changes will continue to honor our core principle: your health data belongs exclusively to you on your device.
