# Kilobite

Shrink any photo or PDF to an exact upload limit — for visas, passports, exams and portals. Everything happens on your device: nothing is uploaded.

## FAQ

**Are my files uploaded anywhere?**
No. All conversion happens on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. The app works fully offline.

**Why is my result marked "Couldn't fit"?**
The size limit is too small for the pixel size you asked for. Lower the pixel size, or pick a bigger size target.

**Are the form presets official?**
Kilobite is not affiliated with any government. The only built-in preset is the US Visa photo (600 × 600 JPEG, up to 240 KB), checked against the U.S. Department of State's digital image requirements page, which the app links to with the date it was checked. Matching the size and dimensions doesn't guarantee acceptance: composition, background and image quality also count, and rules change, so confirm on the official page before you upload. For any other form, save your own preset from what that portal shows. Kilobite has no UK passport or US online passport renewal preset, because those services ask you not to crop or edit the photo yourself.

**How do I restore my subscription?**
Open **Profile → Restore Purchases**.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On your iPhone: **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Kilobite**.

**How do I change the app's language?**
Kilobite follows your iPhone or iPad language. To pick a different one for Kilobite only, open **Profile → Language**, which takes you to **Settings → Kilobite → Language**.
