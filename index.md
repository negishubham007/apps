# Kilobite

Shrink any photo or PDF to an exact upload limit — for visas, passports, exams and portals. Everything happens on your device: nothing is uploaded.

Questions, problems or feature requests: **[shubhamn910@gmail.com](mailto:shubhamn910@gmail.com)**

## FAQ

**Are my files uploaded anywhere?**
No. All conversion happens on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. The app works fully offline.

**Why is my result marked "Couldn't fit"?**
The size limit is too small for the pixel size you asked for. Lower the pixel size, or pick a bigger size target.

**Are the form presets official?**
Every built-in preset links to the official government page its numbers were read from, with the date it was checked. Rules change (exam notices change every cycle), so confirm on the official page before you upload. For portals that don't publish their numbers, save your own preset from what the portal shows.

**How do I restore my subscription?**
Open **Profile → Restore Purchases**.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On your iPhone: **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Kilobite**.

**How do I change the app's language?**
Open **Profile → Language**, choose a language, then close and reopen the app.
