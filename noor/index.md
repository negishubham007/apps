# Noor Widgets — Support & FAQ

Welcome to the official support documentation for **Noor Widgets: Prayer & Quran**.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Why do the prayer times differ slightly from my local mosque?
Islamic prayer times can vary based on the astronomical calculation method used (such as Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, or Dubai IACAD), or because local mosques add manual safety buffers.
- You can change your calculation method in **Settings > Location & Region > Calculation Method**.
- You can fine-tune times to match your local mosque down to the exact minute in **Settings > Match My Mosque (Offsets)**.

#### 2. Why does the app remind me to open Noor once a week?
iOS operating system limits every app to a maximum of **64 scheduled local notifications**. 
Noor schedules 12 full days of alerts (60 notifications for the 5 daily prayers). Opening Noor once every week automatically refills the queue so your adhan alerts never lapse.

#### 3. Why are adhan alert sounds 29.5 seconds?
Apple strictly enforces a maximum 30-second duration for local notification audio on iOS. Our notification chimes and adhan clips are edited to conclude gracefully on a full phrase within 29.5 seconds.

#### 4. How do I restore my Noor Pro purchase?
If you reinstall Noor or switch devices:
1. Open Noor and tap the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. Your subscription will be verified via Apple's StoreKit and restored instantly.
