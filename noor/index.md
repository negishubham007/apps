# Noor Widgets — Support & FAQ

Welcome to the official support documentation for **Noor Widgets: Prayer & Quran**.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Why do the prayer times differ slightly from my local mosque?
Islamic prayer times can vary based on the astronomical calculation method used (such as Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, or Dubai IACAD), or because local mosques add manual safety buffers.
- You can change your calculation method in **Settings > Location & Region > Calculation Method**.
- You can fine-tune times to match your local mosque down to the exact minute in **Settings > Match My Mosque (Offsets)**.

#### 2. Why does the app remind me to open Noor once a week?
iOS operating system limits every app to a maximum of **64 scheduled local notifications**. 
Noor schedules 12 full days of alerts (60 notifications for the 5 daily prayers). Opening Noor once every week automatically refills the queue so your prayer alerts never lapse. If you add reminders before a prayer, they share the same 60 slots, so fewer days are scheduled ahead.

#### 3. What do the prayer alerts sound like?
Prayer alerts use the standard iOS notification sound, or with Noor Pro one of three synthesized chime tones (Makkah, Madinah, Al-Quds). These are chimes, not recordings of the adhan. iOS limits notification sounds to 30 seconds, so each chime tone is 29.5 seconds long. You can turn alerts on or off for each prayer and add a reminder 10, 15 or 30 minutes before it in **Settings > Prayer Alerts**.

#### 4. Is there a free trial?
Yes, the yearly plan starts with 1 free month if your Apple Account hasn't used it before; the paywall shows it only when it applies. You are charged when the free month ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before, in **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions**. Noor reminds you 2 days before the free month ends (turn this off in **Profile**). The monthly plan has no trial.

#### 5. How do I restore my Noor Pro purchase?
If you reinstall Noor or switch devices:
1. Open Noor and tap the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. Your subscription will be verified via Apple's StoreKit and restored instantly.

#### 6. Where do the Quran verses, duas and city list come from?
- Quran Arabic text: [Tanzil Project](https://tanzil.net) (Uthmani text, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0), unchanged.
- Translations: Tanzil.net translation files (English: Sahih International).
- Adhkar: Hisn al-Muslim, with the hadith reference shown under each item.
- Cities: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org) (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0).

The religious content has not yet been checked by a qualified reviewer. If you spot a mistake, please email us.

#### 7. How do I change the app language?
Noor follows your iPhone's language. To use a different language for Noor only, open the iOS **Settings** app > **Apps** > **Noor** > **Language**.
