# Privacy Policy — Noor Widgets

Last updated: October 2026

At **Noor**, privacy is not an afterthought or a marketing phrase — it is fundamental to the app's architecture and design.

### 1. Data Not Collected
Noor does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, location data, or usage metrics. 

### 2. Location Data
- Your device's GPS coordinates are used exclusively on your physical device to compute accurate astronomical prayer times and the Qibla compass bearing towards Mecca.
- **Your location never leaves your phone.** No coordinates, city names, or IP addresses are ever sent to any remote server.
- You can also choose to select a city manually from our offline database without granting location access at all.

### 3. Zero Ads and Third-Party Trackers
- Noor contains **zero third-party software development kits (SDKs)**, zero ad networks, and zero tracking frameworks.
- No behavioral analytics or diagnostic telemetry are collected.

### 4. Purchases and Subscriptions
- Subscriptions to Noor Pro are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple ID.
- We do not receive, process, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address.
