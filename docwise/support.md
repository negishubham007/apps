## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I restore my Docwise Pro purchase?
1. Open **Docwise** and tap the profile button at the top right of the home screen.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. The App Store checks your Apple Account and unlocks Pro again if a subscription is active.

### How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple:
1. Open iOS **Settings**.
2. Tap your name at the top, then **Subscriptions**.
3. Choose **Docwise** and tap **Cancel Subscription**.

Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. Docwise Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription and has no free trial. The free tier lets you try the tools first.

### What is free and what needs Pro?
Free: 2 signed PDF exports, 5 scans (documents and ID cards) and 2 shrinks to a target size. Docwise Pro removes those limits. Your remaining free uses are shown in the Profile screen.

### How do I sign a PDF?
Tap **Sign & Fill**, choose a PDF or a photo, then add your signature, initials, date or text and move them where you want. You can draw a signature with a finger or Apple Pencil, or photograph one on white paper and let Docwise clean up the background. The app keeps up to 3 signatures and 3 initials; adding more replaces the oldest. When you finish, export the PDF. It is flattened, so your signature and text become part of the page.

### How do I fill in a PDF form?
Open the PDF in **Sign & Fill**. If the PDF has form fields, Docwise finds them so you can type into text boxes, tick checkboxes and pick from lists.

### How do I shrink a file to an exact size?
Tap **Shrink File**, choose a PDF or an image, pick **Compress to KB**, and set the limit with the slider, a preset or by typing a number. Docwise compresses the file to fit. **Resize Only** changes the pixel size without a KB target.

### How do passport and visa photos work?
Choose a country or document type, add a photo from the camera, Photos or Files, and Docwise sizes and crops it to that specification. You can replace the background colour, adjust the framing by hand, check the file size, and save a single photo or a print sheet. Photo rules change, so check the official source linked on the screen before you submit. Docwise is not a government service.

### How does scanning work?
Tap **Scan Document** and hold the camera over the page. Docwise finds the edges, straightens the page, and lets you scan more pages. In the Page Manager you can apply a filter, rotate, reorder or delete pages before you save. **Scan ID Card** puts the front and back on one page at real size.

### Does Docwise upload my documents?
No, with one exception: the optional fax feature sends the document you chose to a fax relay when you send a fax. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/apps/docwise/privacy/).

### How do I delete my data?
Delete documents from the document list inside the app, and delete saved signatures in **Profile > Manage Signatures**. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
