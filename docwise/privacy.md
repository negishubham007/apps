# Privacy Policy — Docwise: Sign & Shrink PDF

*Last updated: November 2026*

Docwise is built so that your documents, signatures and photos stay on your device.

---

### 1. What stays on your device
- Scanning, text recognition, signing, form filling, shrinking to a target size, passport and visa photos, merging, splitting and redacting all run on your iPhone or iPad, using Apple frameworks such as Vision, VisionKit, PDFKit and Core Image.
- Docwise has no account system, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking code.
- Your saved signatures, initials and your documents are stored in the app's storage on your device. Signatures are kept with Apple's complete file protection, so they are encrypted while the device is locked.
- Deleting the app removes its local data. You can also delete individual documents and signatures inside the app.

### 2. Optional fax feature: the one thing that leaves your device
Docwise includes an optional fax feature. It is the only part of the app that sends a document over the internet, and only when you choose to send a fax.

- **What is sent:** the PDF you chose to fax, the destination fax number, the page count, and a random identifier created on your device when you first use the app (it is not linked to your name, Apple Account or contacts). When you pick Pro, the request also says that you are a Pro user.
- **Where it goes:** to the Docwise fax relay, a small service that we run on Cloudflare. It passes the fax to a telephone carrier (Telnyx) so it can reach the destination fax machine. Faxes work for destinations in the United States and Canada.
- **How long it is kept:** the PDF is held only while the fax is being sent and is deleted from the relay as soon as the fax is delivered or fails. Records without document content (date, page count, delivery status, random device identifier and page allowance) are kept for up to 30 days so that you can check delivery on your device. Received faxes, if you use a receiving number, are kept for up to 7 days.
- **What the carrier sees:** the document and the number being faxed, because it has to deliver them. Telnyx handles that data under its own terms.
- If you never use the fax feature, Docwise sends no document data anywhere. If the fax service is not available in your version of the app, the feature shows a message instead.

### 3. Purchases
Docwise Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your card number or billing details. The app checks your subscription status with StoreKit, which needs an internet connection when you buy or restore.

### 4. Permissions the app may ask for
- **Camera:** to scan documents and ID cards, photograph a signature, and take passport photos.
- **Face ID:** only if you turn on the app lock in Profile.
- **Calendars:** only if you tap an action that adds a date found in a document to your calendar.
- **Photos:** when you pick photos, the system picker is used and Docwise sees only what you select. The share extension may save converted photos to your library when you choose.

### 5. Children
Docwise does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
