# Residays: Schengen & Tax Days — Support & FAQ

The day counter you can prove. Residays counts the days you spend in each country for visa and tax-residency rules — and every day shows where it came from. Unknown is never guessed.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How does Residays calculate days for each rule?
- **Schengen 90/180 Days:** Evaluates a rolling 180-day window ending on each specific date. Both entry and exit dates count (*Any presence* rule). Days spent in a country where you hold a valid residence permit or national long-stay visa are excluded.
- **UK Statutory Residence Test (SRT):** Uses the *Midnight rule* (present in the UK at 23:59). Evaluated across the official UK tax year (6 April to 5 April).
- **US Substantial Presence Test (SPT):** Weighted formula: current tax year days + 1/3 prior year days + 1/6 two-years-prior days. Requires ≥ 183 weighted days and ≥ 31 days in current year.
- **UAE Tax Residency:** 183 days in 12 consecutive months (or 90 days with qualified ties).
- **US States:** Per-state 183-day statutory residency tracking.

#### Why are some days marked as "Unknown"?
Residays adheres to a strict principle: **accuracy is the product**. Unlike other trackers that guess you stayed home or make false assumptions, Residays only records a day when verified physical evidence (GPS visit, photo EXIF, flight leg, calendar) exists. If you want gaps filled automatically, you can configure an inference rule in Settings (e.g. *Fill with Home Country* or *Fill between same country*).

#### Does Residays give legal or tax advice?
**No.** Residays counts days accurately. It does not decide your tax or visa status. Always export and share your certified report with your qualified tax adviser, accountant, or immigration attorney.

#### How does photo import protect against counting other people's photos?
Residays skips screenshots, images saved from messaging apps (e.g. WhatsApp/Telegram) and any image without camera EXIF data (camera make, model and capture time). Of the rest, only photos from your own cameras — the camera models behind most of your photos — are considered. You are always presented with a grouped review screen before anything is added to your counters.

#### Can I export my data if I cancel Pro?
**Yes, always.** Your travel history belongs to you. The canonical CSV export is free forever and is never locked behind a paywall.
