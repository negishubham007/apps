# Privacy Policy — Residays: Schengen & Tax Days

Last updated: October 2026

At **Residays**, privacy is foundational to our architecture. Travel history and residency records are among the most sensitive personal data you generate.

### 1. Data Not Collected
Residays does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, location coordinates, photos, flight details, or travel logs. Our App Store privacy disclosure is **Data Not Collected**.

### 2. Location & Travel History
- All geographic coordinate lookups are performed **100% on your device** against bundled offline Natural Earth polygon boundaries. No reverse geocoding network calls are made.
- Residays uses Apple's native battery-efficient `CLVisit` and significant location change APIs. Your location fixes and day records never leave your devices.
- If you enable optional iCloud sync, your records sync directly through your personal, private Apple iCloud container (`CloudKit Private Database`). We maintain no servers and have no access to your iCloud data.

### 3. Photos and Media
- When you use the photo import tool, the app scans your camera library locally to extract dates and GPS metadata.
- Photos are never copied, uploaded, or analyzed remotely. Screenshots, messaging app saves, and photos without valid local EXIF camera metadata are automatically ignored.

### 4. Zero Ads and Third-Party SDKs
- Residays contains **zero third-party SDKs**, zero advertising networks, zero analytics trackers, and zero crash-reporting frameworks.
- There are no analytics beacons or remote profiling trackers.

### 5. Purchases & Subscriptions
- In-app subscriptions to Residays Pro are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple ID.
- We do not receive, process, or store your payment details, credit cards, or billing addresses.
