# Jabday: GLP-1 Jab Tracker — Support & FAQ

Log your weekly jab in one tap, see which site you used least recently and when your next jab is due, track your weight in stone, pounds or kilograms, and hand your clinic a clean report. No account, no food database, no dose maths; your data stays on your iPhone.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What does Jabday do?
Jabday is a personal logbook for a medicine you are already prescribed — for example tirzepatide (sold as Mounjaro or Zepbound), semaglutide (Wegovy, Ozempic, Rybelsus), liraglutide, dulaglutide, testosterone, or any medicine you name yourself. You log each jab or tablet, and Jabday shows:
- your next jab day (last logged jab + your schedule, so a late jab moves the next date),
- the injection site you used least recently, from the sites you choose,
- your weight over time, side effects, a short daily check-in and how many doses are left in your pen or box.

Medicine names are shown only so you can recognise what you were prescribed. Jabday is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any manufacturer.

#### Does Jabday suggest or calculate doses?
**No.** Jabday records the dose exactly as your prescriber set it. It never suggests, calculates or converts a dose, has no titration plans and no unit conversions, and makes no promises about weight loss. If you are unsure about a dose, a missed jab or a side effect, talk to your prescriber or pharmacist (in the UK you can also call NHS 111).

#### Can I use stone and pounds?
Yes. Choose **kg**, **st + lb** or **lb** in **Settings**. Stone uses 14 lb to the stone and quarter-pound steps. Jabday stores weight in kilograms and shows it in your unit everywhere, including widgets, Siri and the clinic report.

#### How do reminders work?
Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone — nothing is sent from a server. For each medicine you can turn on a reminder at your chosen time on the jab day, with one follow-up three hours later if you haven't logged it, and one note the next day if it's still not logged. Tap **Log it** on the notification to log the jab at the suggested site. Jabday asks for notification permission only when you turn a reminder on. Reminders are rescheduled every time you log or change something.

#### Widgets and Siri
Add the **Next jab** widget to your Home Screen to see your next jab day and log it with one tap. With Jabday Pro you also get the medium widget (week strip, next site, latest weight, doses left) and the Lock Screen widgets with the next site. You can also say "Log my jab in Jabday", "When is my next jab in Jabday" or "Log my weight in Jabday".

#### Does Jabday work with Apple Health?
Yes, for body weight only, and it's free. Turn it on in **Settings › Apple Health** (or during setup): Jabday reads weights you record elsewhere and saves the weights you log in Jabday to Apple Health. Nothing else is read or written, and Health data never leaves your iPhone.

#### How do I back up or move my data?
Go to **Settings › Export backup** to save a backup file to Files, iCloud Drive or another device. **Settings › Import backup** restores it — you choose whether to merge or replace. **Export CSV** (Progress tab) gives you a spreadsheet of your jabs, weights, side effects and check-ins. Both are free.

Logging every medicine, reminders, the site map, weight with Apple Health, the weight chart, side effects, check-ins, supply count, History, the Log jab widget, Siri, backup and CSV export are free forever.

Jabday Pro adds the clinic report PDF, trends, refill and expiry reminders, the medium and Lock Screen widgets, and more than two active medicines.

- **Monthly:** $4.99 / £3.99 per month (no trial).

Prices in other countries are shown in the app before you subscribe.

To manage or cancel your subscription:
1. Open the iOS **Settings** app.
2. Tap your **Apple Account** name at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions** and select **Jabday**.

Cancelling stops the renewal at the end of the current period; you keep Pro until then. To restore a subscription on a new device, tap **Settings › Restore Purchases** inside Jabday while signed in to the same Apple Account.

#### How do I change the language?
Jabday is available in 14 languages: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish and Simplified Chinese. It follows your iPhone's language. To use a different language for Jabday only, open iOS **Settings › Apps › Jabday › Language**.
