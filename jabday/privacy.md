# Privacy Policy — Jabday: GLP-1 Jab Tracker

Last updated: October 2026

Your medicines, jabs, weight and side effects are sensitive health information. Jabday is built so that all of it stays on your iPhone.

---

### 1. Data Not Collected
Jabday has no account and no sign-in. It does not collect, transmit, store, sell or share your medicines, doses, jabs, injection sites, weights, side effects, check-ins or notes. Our App Store privacy label is **Data Not Collected**.

### 2. On-Device Storage
- Everything you log, and your settings, is stored in one file on your iPhone, in the app's on-device App Group container (`group.com.negishubham.jabday`) so the Jabday widgets can read it.
- We operate no servers, databases or accounts.
- Your data only leaves your device if you choose to export a backup file, a CSV or a clinic report PDF and share it yourself.

### 3. Apple Health
- Apple Health is used only if you turn it on, and only for body weight.
- When it is on, Jabday reads your body-weight samples and saves the weights you log in Jabday to Apple Health, on your device.
- Health data is never sent over the network, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by Jabday.

### 4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
Subscriptions to Jabday Pro are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app only learns whether a subscription is active so it can unlock Pro; payment details and Apple Account information never reach us. Apart from this App Store check, Jabday makes no network connections.

### 5. Notifications
If you turn reminders on, notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. They are never sent from or through a server.

### 6. No Ads, No Analytics
Jabday contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

### 7. Export & Delete
- **Export:** **Settings › Export backup** (JSON) or **Export CSV** on the Progress tab, any time, free.
- **Delete:** **Settings › Delete All Data** removes everything Jabday stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. Weights already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

### 8. Children's Privacy
Jabday is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone. All data remains on the device.

### 9. Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
