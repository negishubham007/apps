# Living Dawn: Support and FAQ

Living Dawn is an alarm clock where the sky rises with you. Your alarm rings through silent mode, keeps ringing every 2 minutes until you finish a short wake-up task, and the task lifts the sun on the wake-up screen. Free to try, no account, the price is shown first.

---

### Frequently asked questions

#### What if I need to stop it?
You can always stop each ring with the iPhone's own controls: the slider on the alarm, or a button on the side or volume buttons. Inside the app, tap **I can't do this now**, hold the button for 60 seconds and type the code shown. This ends that morning's alarm without the task. It is saved in your History and it breaks your streak. Nobody is ever trapped, for example while driving or in class.

#### Does it really ring when the app is closed or the phone is on silent?
Living Dawn uses the iPhone's own alarm system, so the alarms belong to iOS, not to the app. They ring with the app closed and in silent mode and Focus. After a ring, open Living Dawn to do your task. A locked iPhone needs Face ID or your passcode before the app opens. The Open button on the alarm can fail on some iPhones, so if it does nothing, open the app from its icon.

#### How do the backup alarms work?
When you set an alarm, Living Dawn also sets backup alarms 2 minutes apart (8 by default; Pro can set 4 to 12). If you stop an alarm without finishing the task, the next backup rings. When you finish the task, the app cancels the rest. Backups are set ahead of time, so nothing depends on the app running at the moment of the alarm.

What stops them: sliding the alarm to stop, pressing a hardware button (this stops every alarm that is ringing at that moment), switching the phone off, a flat battery, turning off all alarms in Settings, or deleting the app.

#### Keep a second alarm for flights and exams
Any alarm app can fail, and so can a phone with a flat battery. For anything critical, such as a flight or an exam, set a second alarm on another device.

#### How to remove Living Dawn
1. Open Living Dawn, then Settings, then **Turn off all alarms**.
2. Delete the app as usual.

When you delete the app, iOS removes its alarms. (We check this on real iPhones before each release.)

#### How do I turn all alarms off at once?
Settings, then **Turn off all alarms**. Every alarm is switched off and removed from the iPhone's alarm system. Your alarms stay in the app so you can turn them on again.

#### What is free and what is Pro?
Free forever: maths, typing a code, steps and shake tasks, 2 alarms with the backup alarms, 5 loud sounds and your streak. Pro (monthly or yearly): camera exercises, photo tasks, talking to the dawn, several tasks on one alarm, the wake-up check a few minutes later, blocking apps until you are up, unlimited alarms, all sounds, your own voice and files, achievements and more widgets, four extra dawn scenes (ocean, mountains, desert, forest), a 30-day chart of wake time and finished tasks, and optional iCloud sync. The yearly plan includes a free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app, taken from the App Store.

If Pro ends, your Pro tasks become Maths, extra alarms are switched off (never deleted), the classic dawn scene is shown (your scene choice is kept for when Pro returns), iCloud sync pauses, and every alarm keeps ringing. Nothing is lost.

#### Do the steps count if I just shake the phone?
Steps count while your iPhone sees you walking, but shaking can still fool it. The steps task is meant to get you to another room, not to prove it. The photo and camera tasks use the live camera; a photo of a screen can fool them.

#### Does the camera record me?
No. Exercise counting and photo checks run on your iPhone. Pictures and video are never saved or sent. (The one exception is your own target photo, a small picture kept on the iPhone so the alarm can compare against it.)

#### Why does the talk task need Apple Intelligence?
Talking to the dawn uses the language model that is built into some iPhones (iPhone 15 Pro and newer, with Apple Intelligence turned on, in supported languages). On other iPhones, or in other languages, the same task asks you to say a sentence and some numbers, checked without a model. Everything runs on the device.

#### Why does the sunrise line show "Your dawn"?
The real sunrise time is shown when your alarm is close to the real sunrise at your place. Otherwise the wake-up screen shows a composed dawn. The sunrise is calculated on your iPhone from the date and your approximate place; nothing is sent anywhere.

#### Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The App Store privacy label is **Data Not Collected**.

The one option that moves data off the iPhone is **iCloud sync** (Pro, Settings, off by default). When you turn it on, your alarms, settings, history and streak go to your own iCloud and to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. They never go to us, and we cannot see them. Recorded sounds and your target photo are not synced.

#### How does iCloud sync work?
Turn on **Sync with iCloud** in Settings (Pro). The newest change wins. If two devices changed alarms at the same time, the newer settings win but no alarm is thrown away, and history from both devices is combined. Each device arms its own alarms with the iPhone's alarm system, so a device that has received an alarm will ring too, and the task finished on one device is recognised on the others once they have synced; until then both can ring. You need to be signed in to iCloud on each device. **Delete All Data** also removes the copy in your iCloud.

#### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: **Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions, Living Dawn**. Cancelling there stops renewal. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged. Living Dawn can remind you two days before the trial ends.

#### How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in the app and tap **Restore Purchases**.
