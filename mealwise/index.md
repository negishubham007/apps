# Mealwise: Support & FAQ

Mealwise plans a week of dinners to your budget and builds one shopping list, grouped by aisle, with an estimated total.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How accurate are the prices?
They are estimates for a typical supermarket, shown with a likely range, and are not a quote from any store. Tap any price on the shopping list to enter what you really pay: Mealwise remembers it. After shopping, enter or scan your receipt total under Shopping and later estimates adjust to your shop. You can also choose Discount, Standard or Premium under Preferences.

#### Does Mealwise handle allergies?
Diet filters and the "foods to leave out" list follow the ingredients listed in each recipe. They do not cover cross-contamination or product variations. Always check product labels if you have an allergy. Mealwise gives no medical or nutrition advice.

#### Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Mealwise stores everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no analytics. If you use the shared household list, its items go through your own iCloud to the people you invite, never to us. The App Store privacy disclosure is *Data Not Collected*.

#### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Mealwise**. Cancelling there stops the renewal at the end of the current period; you keep Pro until then.

#### I restored my device / bought a new iPhone — where is my Pro?
Open Mealwise → **Settings › Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account. If the subscription was purchased on another Apple Account, sign in with that account first.

#### How do I export my data?
**Settings › Export backup** writes a backup file you can save to Files or send to another device. **Settings › Import backup** brings it back — you choose whether to merge or replace.

#### Something isn't working — how do I report it?
Write to **shubhamn910@gmail.com** from the address you used on the App Store. Include the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what you did before the problem appeared.
