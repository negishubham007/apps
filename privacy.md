# Kilobite — Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 October 2026

## Files and local storage

Photos and documents are processed on your device. The app does not upload your files to its own servers. Conversion works offline. Imported copies, previews and converted outputs may remain in the app's temporary storage until the app or operating system removes them. Exported copies remain wherever you save or share them; that destination's privacy practices apply.

Settings, saved presets and free-save usage are stored locally, in storage shared only by the app and its Share extension on this device. Removing the app removes its local app data, except copies you exported elsewhere.

Converted JPEGs remove location and camera metadata. PDFs can retain original page content, annotations and document information; do not assume PDF processing anonymizes a document.

## Accounts and tracking

The app has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. We do not receive your photos, documents or saved presets.

## Purchases

Apple handles purchases and payment details. Purchase, restore and subscription verification may require an internet connection. The app reads purchase entitlement information to enable paid features.
